Abstract
Keywords: Restaurant Automation, ESP32, IoT Paging System, Customer Experience, Order Management. This project presents an IoT-based food paging system designed for self-service restaurants. It replaces traditional display boards with portable, wireless buzzers (ESP32-based) to instantly notify customers when their orders are ready, improving queue management and customer satisfaction.

Introduction
In self-service restaurants, managing customer queues and order pickups is challenging. Traditional digital display boards often go unnoticed, leading to cold food and crowded waiting areas. This project introduces a smart paging system consisting of an offline admin dashboard for staff and a portable alert device for customers.

Related Study / Literature Review
Current solutions rely on large digital screens where token numbers are displayed, or manual shouting of order numbers.

Drawback of Displays: Customers must constantly monitor the screen, limiting their ability to relax.
Drawback of Audio Systems: In noisy restaurant environments, PA systems are ineffective and disruptive.
Existing Pagers: Commercial pagers exist but are often expensive, proprietary, and lack customizable, centralized web dashboard integration.
Problem Statement (4 Demerits of Current Systems)
Current self-service notification methods suffer from four critical demerits:

Poor Visibility: Customers frequently miss their token numbers on display boards.
Crowded Waiting Areas: Unsure customers crowd the pickup counter, causing congestion.
Food Quality Degradation: Delayed pickups lead to food getting cold or losing its intended quality.
Poor Customer Experience: Customers waste time monitoring screens instead of socializing or relaxing at their tables.
Objectives (Derived from Demerits)
Provide instant, unmissable notifications (Buzzer, LED, Vibration) to solve poor visibility.
Allow customers to wait anywhere in the restaurant to reduce counter congestion.
Ensure timely order collection to maintain optimal food quality.
Enhance customer convenience by providing a dedicated, portable alert device.
Methodology
The system is divided into two primary components:

Hardware (ESP32 Device):
Acts as the client/pager.
Equipped with a Buzzer (GPIO2) for audio alerts, an LED for visual alerts, and a vibration motor.
Includes a "Mute/Return" button for the customer to turn off the alert.
Continuously polls the local server via Wi-Fi to check order status.
Software (Admin Interface):
A Flask-based web dashboard (Python/SQLite).
Allows admins to register unique devices, link orders (token no., dine-in/takeaway) to active devices, and trigger "Order Ready" alerts with a single click.

Future Upgrades
Rechargeable Station: Develop a centralized docking station to charge multiple pagers simultaneously.
Range Extension: Implement LoRa or mesh networking for larger restaurants where standard Wi-Fi might not reach.
Analytics Dashboard: Add features to track average wait times and peak order hours to help owners optimize kitchen efficiency.
